Comic Anthology Submission Guidelines – Twisted History

Simple But Complex is currently accepting comic anthology submissions for our upcoming project, Twisted History. We invite indie comic creators, writers, and artists to submit original short comics for consideration in a professionally published anthology.

This year’s theme, Twisted History, invites you to take a real historical moment, figure, or era and reinterpret it through a supernatural, mythical, or eerie lens. The goal is creative transformation—start grounded in reality, then push into the unexpected.

Below you’ll find technical requirements, story expectations, deadlines, submission links, and official documentation. Please review all sections carefully before submitting.

Comic Submission Requirements

  • Page Count: 8–16 pages total, including cover
  • Trim Size: 5.5" x 8.5"
  • Resolution: 300 DPI or higher
  • Color: Color or black & white accepted
  • File Format: One combined, print-ready PDF
  • Originality: No AI-generated art or writing

Story Guidelines

  • Inspired by a real historical moment, figure, or era
  • Includes a supernatural, mythical, or horror-based twist
  • Maintains a PG-13 tone

Prizes & Contributor Copies

  • 1st Place: $100
  • 2nd Place: $80
  • 3rd Place: $70
  • 4th Place: $50

Winners: One signed contributor copy.
All accepted contributors: Digital contributor copy.

At this time, contributors are not paid page rates for participation in this anthology. Compensation is provided through cash prizes, contributor copies, publication credit, and inclusion in a professionally distributed release.

Submission Timeline

  • Submissions Open: January 19, 2026
  • Submission Deadline: March 2, 2026
  • Judging Period: March 3–9, 2026
  • Winners Announced: March 14, 2026

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept first-time comic creators?
Yes. New and experienced creators are welcome.

Can I submit previously published work?
No. All submissions must be original and unpublished.

Do creators retain rights to their work?
Yes. Creators retain ownership unless otherwise stated in writing.

How long does the review process take?
Most submissions are reviewed within 2–6 weeks.

Is this a paid opportunity?
This anthology does not offer page rates. Contributors are compensated through cash prizes, publication credit, contributor copies, and participation in a professionally published anthology.
