Twisted History Anthology – Submissions Closed

Submissions for the Twisted History Comic Anthology are now officially closed. Thank you to everyone who took the time to create, submit, and share their work with Simple But Complex.

We received submissions from talented creators around the world, and the creativity displayed throughout this contest made the judging process extremely competitive.

Congratulations to the selected creators and contest winners. Their stories will be featured in the upcoming Twisted History Anthology.

Even if your submission was not selected this time, we encourage you to keep creating and to watch for future anthology opportunities with Simple But Complex.

Anthology Theme

The Twisted History anthology challenged creators to reinterpret real historical moments, figures, and eras through supernatural, mythical, or eerie twists. Stories began with recognizable historical foundations and pushed them into unexpected and imaginative territory.

Original Submission Requirements

  • Page Count: 8–16 pages total including cover
  • Trim Size: 5.5" × 8.5"
  • Resolution: 300 DPI minimum
  • Format: Single print-ready PDF
  • Color: Color or Black & White accepted

Submission Timeline

  • Submissions Open: January 19, 2026
  • Submission Deadline: March 2, 2026
  • Judging Period: March 3–9, 2026
  • Winners Announced: March 14, 2026

About the Anthology

Selected creators will appear in the upcoming Twisted History Anthology, published by Simple But Complex. The anthology highlights emerging and independent comic creators exploring imaginative reinterpretations of history.

Release information and availability will be announced soon.
